References

The formulas are on the Methods page.

The methods this package implements, and the work it follows. Each entry says where in the package it is used.

Missing-data mechanisms

  • Rubin, D. B. (1976). Inference and missing data. Biometrika, 63(3), 581–592. https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/63.3.581 — the MCAR / MAR / MNAR distinction the diagnostics are read against.
  • Little, R. J. A., & Rubin, D. B. (2019). Statistical Analysis with Missing Data (3rd ed.). Wiley. https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119482260 — the standard reference for the field, including listwise deletion (missingrows, missingdrop) and maximum-likelihood estimation under missing data.

Little's MCAR test (missingmcar)

  • Little, R. J. A. (1988). A test of missing completely at random for multivariate data with missing values. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 83(404), 1198–1202. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1988.10478722 — the test statistic and its χ² reference distribution.
  • Dempster, A. P., Laird, N. M., & Rubin, D. B. (1977). Maximum likelihood from incomplete data via the EM algorithm. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 39(1), 1–22. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1977.tb01600.x — the EM estimates of the mean and covariance the test compares against.
  • Anderson, T. W. (1957). Maximum likelihood estimates for a multivariate normal distribution when some observations are missing. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 52(278), 200–203. https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1957.10501379 — the closed-form estimates for a monotone pattern, which the test suite checks EM against.
  • Press, W. H., Teukolsky, S. A., Vetterling, W. T., & Flannery, B. P. (2007). Numerical Recipes: The Art of Scientific Computing (3rd ed.), §6.2. Cambridge University Press. — §6.2, the series and continued-fraction evaluation of the incomplete gamma function behind the χ² p-values; §6.4, the incomplete beta function behind the t p-values of missingpredictors.
  • Lanczos, C. (1964). A precision approximation of the gamma function. Journal of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, Series B: Numerical Analysis, 1(1), 86–96. https://doi.org/10.1137/0701008 — the log-gamma used by the incomplete beta function.

Predictors of missingness (missingpredictors)

  • Welch, B. L. (1947). The generalization of 'Student's' problem when several different population variances are involved. Biometrika, 34(1–2), 28–35. https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/34.1-2.28 — the unequal-variance t-test and its degrees of freedom.
  • Pearson, K. (1900). On the criterion that a given system of deviations from the probable in the case of a correlated system of variables is such that it can be reasonably supposed to have arisen from random sampling. The London, Edinburgh, and Dublin Philosophical Magazine and Journal of Science, 50(302), 157–175. https://doi.org/10.1080/14786440009463897 — the χ² test of independence.
  • Cramér, H. (1946). Mathematical Methods of Statistics. Princeton University Press. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400883868 — Cramér's V.
  • Cohen, J. (1988). Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioral Sciences (2nd ed.). Lawrence Erlbaum; reprinted 2013, Routledge. https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203771587 — the standardized mean difference and its conventional 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.8 benchmarks.

Patterns and co-occurrence (missingpatterns, missingcooccurrence, order=:cluster)

  • van Buuren, S., & Groothuis-Oudshoorn, K. (2011). mice: Multivariate imputation by chained equations in R. Journal of Statistical Software, 45(3). https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v045.i03 — md.pattern(), the pattern table missingpatterns reproduces.
  • Lex, A., Gehlenborg, N., Strobelt, H., Vuillemot, R., & Pfister, H. (2014). UpSet: Visualization of intersecting sets. IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 20(12), 1983–1992. https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2014.2346248 — the frequency bars beside each pattern.
  • Yule, G. U. (1912). On the methods of measuring association between two attributes. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, 75(6), 579–652. https://doi.org/10.2307/2340126 — the ϕ coefficient of two binary attributes, here the missingness masks.
  • Jaccard, P. (1912). The distribution of the flora in the alpine zone. New Phytologist, 11(2), 37–50. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-8137.1912.tb05611.x — the Jaccard index of the missing-row sets.
  • Hahsler, M., Hornik, K., & Buchta, C. (2008). Getting things in order: An introduction to the R package seriation. Journal of Statistical Software, 25(3). https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v025.i03 — seriation, of which order=:cluster is a greedy nearest-neighbour instance.

Calendar grouping (period=:week)

  • ISO 8601-1:2019. Date and time — Representations for information interchange — Part 1: Basic rules. International Organization for Standardization. — the week numbering and week-based year.

Sentinel detection (missingsentinels) has no reference: its rules are this package's own heuristic, written out on the Methods page.

Related software

  • Tierney, N., & Cook, D. (2023). Expanding tidy data principles to facilitate missing data exploration, visualization and assessment of imputations. Journal of Statistical Software, 105(7). https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v105.i07 — naniar, the R package for the same exploration, including a Little's test (mcar_test).
